| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Upfront | The fee charged when a customer buys campaigns, typically $1,500/campaign (or $2,000–2,500 flat for no-success-fee customers), invoiced monthly or per agreed pilot schedule ✅ FM confirmed, Aug 2026 |
| Per Meeting | The success fee (see Block 10 for exact figure) invoiced when a booked meeting is attended by the prospect ✅ FM confirmed, Aug 2026 |
| Campaign | A single personalized outbound marketing package (e.g. a custom surfboard, board game, branding iron) tied to a specific prospect/account, tracked via a short code (e.g. "SLV19", "RIP-xx") in Wildcard's internal product DB ✅ FM confirmed, Aug 2026 |
| Pilot | A 3–4 month initial engagement (usually 30–40 campaigns) before converting to a 6–12 month recurring engagement ✅ FM confirmed, Aug 2026 |
| P&L line naming conventions | Standard terminology applies — no custom naming convention in use ✅ FM confirmed, Aug 2026 |
| Category naming conventions | ❌ MISSING — not yet established with new accounting team |
| Abbreviations | ❌ MISSING — none documented beyond terms above |
